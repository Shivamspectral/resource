Rules & Regulations

Safe, transparent, responsible exchange.

Platform rules are designed to protect organizations, recipients and the resources being transferred.

VERSION 2.4 · 2026
01

Eligibility & verification

Organizations must provide accurate identity, contact and operating information. ReSourceX may require additional verification before an exchange.

02

Accurate listings

Every listing must state the correct quantity, condition, location, expiry or availability, packaging and relevant handling information.

03

Healthcare materials

Medical products must be lawfully transferable, appropriately packaged, traceable where required, and within usable dates. Expired or compromised products are prohibited.

04

Research & chemical materials

Regulated, hazardous or controlled materials require appropriate authorization and documentation. Prohibited substances cannot be listed or exchanged.

05

Transport & chain of custody

The sender and recipient must agree on safe handling, pickup, transport and receipt. Required records should be retained by the responsible parties.

06

Fraud, misuse & safety

False listings, unsafe materials, impersonation, payment fraud, harassment and attempts to bypass verification can result in listing removal or account suspension.

07

Data & privacy

Use organization and contact data only for legitimate exchange purposes. Do not publish personal information that is unnecessary for a transaction.

08

Disputes & reporting

Report inaccurate listings or unsafe behavior promptly. Keep evidence and transaction records. ReSourceX can pause a match while a report is reviewed.

Compliance acknowledgement

By publishing or requesting a resource, your organization confirms it will follow applicable laws, safety requirements and these platform rules.
